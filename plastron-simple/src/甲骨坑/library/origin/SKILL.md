# origin — the starting point

boot contract: `createInitialState()` + `hydrate(state, [], [])` and
`元.view` paints freespace at `元.mount` ("#app"). one visible cel —
the origin, whose value is the readme — above an invisible locked
floor. everything else is made by typing formulas.

- entry gesture: type at the open origin, press enter → a new cel
  `c1, c2, …` (segment "freespace") appears beside it. `=` is infix,
  `(` is s-expr, anything else is a literal.
- click a cel to open it; its source loads into the box for editing.
  commit an empty box to delete the cel (and sweep its bloom).
- `=load("name")` loads a library; `=cels("name")` lists one.
- the view (元.view) is UNLOCKED and minimal by design — rework it in
  place; it is just another formula cel painting through plastron-dom.
